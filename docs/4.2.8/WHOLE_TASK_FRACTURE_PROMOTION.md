# Whole-task failure promotion

A failed simple execution or planning stage can now choose fracture at its
researched ten- or twenty-failure boundary. The controller captures the exact
failed job/intent, native pivot result, dossier and original lineage. Repeated
polls reuse that capture; they do not reopen an old physical job.

The task returns through SRS revision, three-channel research, different-model
plan review and WBS. Complexity 8–10 still requires the authenticated owner SRS
approval. The revised SRS preserves the original requirement IDs/text, release
criteria and retained reasoning floor. The reviewed WBS supplies deliverable
paths when a failed simple job never produced any; the controller does not
invent them. Its final stage is `FRACTURE_PROMOTION_READY`, so ordinary WBS child
materialization cannot accidentally create fresh quality budgets.

The existing fracture planner and independent exact-plan reviewer then prepare
the individually assessed implementation, assembly and final-test graph. Only
the durable registered graph is projected into `MICROTASKS_READY`. Every child
uses its registered member identity and the original shared quality root. The
whole graph has no fabricated original-microtask alias. Its final scope remains
subject to normal artifact verification and parent-release checks.

SRS/planning control attempts have a separate bounded quality ledger so the
original research gate stays closed. Immutable economic group links charge
their dispatches to the original aggregate dispatch ceiling and oldest expiry.
The live reservation guard includes those groups, preserves finite minima and
stops queued controls when the original boundary closes or locks. A successful
fracture keeps all ten/twenty prior failures; it never resets the thirty-failure
lock, quota accounting or four execution seats.

Source fixtures cover simple and initial-planning failure promotion, exact
reviewed WBS paths, owner approval holds, requirement/floor rejection, original
failure retention, direct registered dispatch, cancellation, restart and an
interruption after graph commit but before the journal projection. Temporary
SQLite and inert native receipts do not establish host deployment acceptance.
